# 3D Snow Bike Racing Game Prompt

Create a fun, highly polished 3D Snow Bike Racing game with a bright, colorful, cartoonish visual style and exciting arcade gameplay.

## CORE GAMEPLAY

The player controls a powerful snow bike/motocross bike racing downhill from the peak of a massive snowy mountain toward the finish line.

The gameplay should feel fast, responsive, funny, and satisfying. The player must:

- Accelerate downhill
- Steer left and right
- Avoid or jump over obstacles
- Perform wheelies
- Lift the front of the bike
- Jump from ramps
- Perform aerial stunts and flips
- Land safely
- Collect coins/rewards
- Reach checkpoints
- Cross the finish line before time runs out

## BIKE CONTROLS

Make controls simple and mobile-friendly:

- Left button → steer left
- Right button → steer right
- Throttle → accelerate
- Brake → slow down
- Jump → launch the bike from suitable terrain
- Front-wheel lift / Wheelie → raise the front of the bike
- Stunt button → perform aerial tricks
- Boost → temporary speed burst

The bike should have realistic but arcade-friendly physics. During jumps, the player should have control over the bike's rotation and be able to perform:

- Backflip
- Front flip
- 360 spin
- Wheelie
- Long jump
- Combo stunts

Successful tricks should award bonus points and coins.

## EXCITING OBSTACLES

Create many different downhill obstacles:

- Snow ramps
- Broken bridges
- Wooden barriers
- Fallen trees
- Snow-covered rocks
- Ice patches
- Deep snow
- Snowballs
- Moving obstacles
- Narrow mountain paths
- Gaps between cliffs
- Collapsing bridges
- Avalanche sections
- Giant jumps
- Ice tunnels
- Snow-covered village obstacles

Obstacle placement should change between races so the player cannot simply memorize one route.

## RACE STRUCTURE

Each race begins at a spectacular mountain peak.

The player continuously travels downhill toward the finish line.

Include:

- Starting countdown: 3, 2, 1, GO!
- Checkpoints
- Race timer
- Distance-to-finish indicator
- Position/rank
- Speedometer
- Coins
- Stunt score
- Boost meter
- Finish-line celebration

Create multiple race difficulties:

1. Easy
2. Medium
3. Hard
4. Extreme

As difficulty increases, increase speed, obstacle density, jumps, cliffs and environmental challenges.

## DIFFERENT SNOW ENVIRONMENTS

Every race should feel visually different even though the overall theme is snowy mountains.

Create multiple locations:

1. Sunrise Peaks
   
   - Golden sunrise
   - Orange/pink sky
   - Long mountain shadows

2. Frozen Night
   
   - Dark blue environment
   - Bright moon
   - Stars
   - Aurora borealis
   - Glowing ice

3. Snowy Village
   
   - Wooden cabins
   - Pine trees
   - Bridges
   - Warm glowing windows
   - Mountain village atmosphere

4. Blizzard Valley
   
   - Heavy snowfall
   - Strong wind
   - Reduced visibility
   - Snow particles

5. Sunset Ridge
   
   - Orange/red sunset
   - Dramatic mountain silhouettes
   - Long downhill jumps

6. Ice Canyon
   
   - Huge blue ice walls
   - Frozen waterfalls
   - Ice tunnels
   - Narrow tracks

7. Avalanche Mountain
   
   - Falling snow
   - Rolling snowballs
   - Avalanche sequences
   - High-speed escape sections

8. Northern Lights
   
   - Colorful aurora
   - Night sky
   - Glowing checkpoints
   - Magical icy atmosphere

Use different camera angles, lighting, weather, skyboxes and environmental compositions for every location.

## VISUAL STYLE

Use a bright premium 3D cartoonish arcade style.

Visual references:

- Colorful
- Clean
- High contrast
- Charming
- Slightly exaggerated proportions
- Detailed 3D environments
- Smooth animations
- Glossy snow
- Beautiful lighting
- Vibrant blue skies
- Colorful bikes
- Fun exaggerated stunt effects

Avoid dull, realistic simulator graphics. The game should immediately look fun and approachable.

## BIKE DESIGN

Create several unlockable snow bikes.

Each bike should have a different personality:

- Speed Bike → extremely fast
- Control Bike → easy handling
- Stunt Bike → excellent aerial rotation
- Power Bike → heavy and powerful

Allow customization:

- Bike color
- Wheels
- Decals
- Rider helmet
- Rider outfit
- Exhaust effects

Bike upgrades:

- Speed
- Acceleration
- Handling
- Jump
- Stunt control
- Boost
- Stability

## ENGAGING ANIMATIONS

Add polished animations everywhere.

Bike:

- Suspension compression
- Wheel rotation
- Tire deformation
- Snow spraying from tires
- Exhaust animation
- Bike leaning during turns
- Front wheel lifting
- Rider body movement
- Rider reacting during jumps
- Landing impact
- Crash animation

Environment:

- Falling snow
- Blowing snow
- Snow particles
- Trees moving in wind
- Birds
- Flying snow
- Avalanches
- Ice cracking
- Snowballs rolling downhill

Make jumps and landings especially satisfying with camera shake, particle effects and slow-motion moments.

## STUNT SYSTEM

Create a combo-based stunt system.

Example:

Wheelie → +100
Jump → +100
Backflip → +300
Front Flip → +300
360 Spin → +500
Long Jump → +250
Perfect Landing → +500

Combining tricks should create a multiplier:

2x → 3x → 4x → 5x

Show large colorful floating score numbers during stunts.

## BOOST SYSTEM

Collect boost energy by:

- Performing tricks
- Collecting boost items
- Driving through special gates
- Maintaining high speed

When BOOST is activated:

- Bike accelerates dramatically
- Camera pulls back slightly
- Motion blur increases
- Snow particles intensify
- Exhaust glows
- Music becomes more energetic

## AUDIO

Create exciting arcade-style audio.

Include:

- Powerful snow bike engine
- Engine RPM changing with speed
- Tire sounds on snow
- Ice sliding sounds
- Jump whoosh
- Landing impact
- Boost sound
- Coin collection
- Stunt sound effects
- Crash sounds
- Checkpoint sound
- Finish-line celebration
- Avalanche rumble
- Wind
- Snowstorm ambience

Music should be energetic, fun and adventurous with different tracks for different environments.

Music should dynamically become more intense during:

- High speed
- Final section of the race
- Avalanche sequences
- Boost
- Final jump

## CAMERA

Use a dynamic third-person chase camera.

Camera should:

- Follow the bike smoothly
- Pull back slightly at high speed
- Move closer during stunts
- Shake slightly during heavy landings
- Use dramatic angles during huge jumps
- Provide a short slow-motion camera moment for spectacular stunts

Do not make the camera uncomfortable or overly shaky.

## GAME PROGRESSION

Create a progression system:

Race → Earn coins → Unlock bikes → Upgrade bike → Unlock harder mountains → Discover new environments.

Add:

- Stars for race performance
- Coins
- XP
- Stunt score
- Race rewards
- Bike unlocks
- Mountain unlocks

Three-star system:
⭐ Finish the race
⭐⭐ Complete under target time
⭐⭐⭐ Complete under target time + high stunt score

Create a polished mobile game interface.

Main menu:

- PLAY

- SETTINGS

During race:

## FUN GAME FEEL

The most important goal is FUN.

Every jump should feel rewarding.

Every landing should have satisfying feedback.

Every stunt should feel exciting.

The player should constantly encounter something interesting: a ramp, obstacle, jump, shortcut, stunt opportunity, collectible, avalanche or spectacular mountain view.

Add occasional hidden shortcuts and alternate paths so skilled players can discover faster routes.

## FINISH LINE

Make the finish line visually spectacular.

When the player finishes:

- Fireworks
- Confetti
- Snow particles
- Crowd cheering
- Bike victory animation
- Rider celebrates
- Final score appears
- Coins and XP are awarded
- Star rating is shown

## PERFORMANCE

Optimize the game for Android mobile devices.

Target:

- Smooth 60 FPS where hardware allows
- Responsive touch controls
- Fast loading
- Optimized particle effects
- Efficient environment rendering
- Scalable graphics quality
- Low memory usage

The game should feel polished enough for a Google Play Store arcade racing game.

## OVERALL EXPERIENCE

The final game should feel like a combination of:

Fast downhill snow racing + motocross stunts + colorful arcade gameplay + spectacular mountain environments.

Make the game immediately playable and fun within the first 10 seconds, while providing enough progression, upgrades, environments and stunt mechanics to keep players coming back.

Use the provided concept image as the visual direction/reference for the game's colorful 3D snow-bike racing identity, but create original game assets and environments rather than copying the image.
